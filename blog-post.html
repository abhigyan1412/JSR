Preparing Enterprise Data Infrastructure for DPDPA 2023

Published January 2026 · By Technical Advisory Team

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) has shifted regulatory compliance from a periodic audit item to a continuous technical requirement.

The Technical Mandate

Data Fiduciaries must ensure that personal data processing is backed by explicit consent logs, robust encryption at rest and in transit, and immediate breach notification capabilities to the Data Protection Board.

Core Steps to Readiness

  • Automated PII data discovery across multi-cloud and on-premise storage arrays.
  • Deployment of enterprise Consent Management Platforms (CMP) with tamper-evident audit trails.
  • System integration of Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) and Data Loss Prevention (DLP) agents.
