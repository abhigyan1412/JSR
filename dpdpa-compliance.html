Regulatory Overview

Understanding the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

The DPDPA 2023 establishes stringent requirements for Data Fiduciaries handling personal data of Indian citizens, with penalties reaching up to ₹250 Crore per violation.

Notice & Lawful Consent

Mandatory clear, itemized consent notices in English and 22 scheduled Indian languages prior to processing personal data.

Data Principal Rights

Enabling citizens to access, correct, erase data, and withdraw consent seamlessly through accessible digital interfaces.

Breach Notification & Governance

Immediate incident reporting to the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals without delay during data compromises.

Frequently Asked Questions

DPDPA 2023 Compliance FAQ

Who does the DPDPA 2023 apply to?

It applies to any entity (Data Fiduciary) processing digital personal data within India, or processing data outside India if offering goods or services to individuals in India.

What are the non-compliance penalties?

Penalties can reach up to ₹250 Crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches, and up to ₹200 Crore for failure to notify breaches.

How long does a typical compliance implementation take?

Depending on data complexity, an initial gap assessment takes 2–4 weeks, followed by 8–12 weeks of technical and workflow remediation.
